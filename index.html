How we build

Teams first

Projects are written for shared work: reviewable, repeatable and documented, not just one developer's setup.

Open by default

Code, issues and roadmaps live on GitHub. Discussions are open to anyone.

Not tied to one modeling method

The name nods to dimensional modeling. Projects can use star schemas, snowflake schemas or other approaches, whichever fits the data.

Who it's for

BI developers

Skills and starters that cut setup time on your own projects.

BI teams

Shared conventions and tooling that a whole team can adopt.

Consultants

Reusable building blocks for client work, including migrations.

Follow along or contribute

Roadmaps and decisions are discussed in the open. Join a discussion, open an issue, or propose a project.
